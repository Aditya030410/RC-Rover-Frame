# RC ROVER FRAME !!

So, It is a frame for your RC rovers, u can attach controller PCB and motors and other stuff in the central hub and the compartments beside tyres. It can be 3d printed, u can attach ur own tyres i just put them for referance !! I made appropriate holes and spaces for components and a chamber at the end of the leg to place a motor and also made holes there also for wiring and connection. If u look at the upper body it as a depth type curve inside it, it can be customized further to attach a camera there !!

## How I made it? 

Actually, I got some knowledge about CAD from making FPV drone Frame and a propeller previously by watching tutorials and now i had enough knowledge to put in my imagination, i wanted to do some CAD for atlantis program by hackclub but didn't have any good idea or a tutorial for a rover frame, so i just came up with this and made it !! 


## Why I made it?
I was curious to make something like the things i am working on like the FPV drone frame and propeller because i had to do CAD for the atlantis program as I said before so i just hopped on!!
